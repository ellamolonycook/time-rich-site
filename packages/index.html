How it works

From yes to recap in about five weeks.

Pick the room and the tier

A call or an email. We confirm which audience you want, the date, the room size and what your category exclusivity covers.

You go on the page

Your name and logo go live within a day, promotion opens across the newsletter and both social channels, and registration starts filling.

We build the run of show around you

Stage moment, floor team, judging seat and the offer to the room, all agreed a fortnight out. You send assets and a contact for the day.

The event runs

We handle venue, production, staffing, food and photography. Your team turns up and talks to people.

Recap within seven days

Attendance, products shipped, sign-ups, opt-in data, the full content library and the reach numbers, in one page.
