Drafting your first AI Agent Personas

Learn how to define clear boundaries, knowledge bases, and interaction styles to prevent your AI agents from hallucinating or going off-script.

Loom Video Embed

Why Personas Matter

When you deploy an AI agent into your company's Slack or customer-facing portal, it needs to know exactly who it is and what it is allowed to do. Without a strict persona, an LLM will default to being a generic, helpful assistant - which often leads to it answering questions outside its domain or making up company policies.

System Prompt Template
You are an expert customer support agent for [Company]. Your tone is professional, concise, and deeply empathetic. Core Rules: 1. Never invent policies. If you don't know, tell the user you will escalate to a human. 2. Only answer questions related to [Product Domain]. 3. Keep responses under 3 sentences unless explaining a technical fix.

Building the Knowledge Base

The persona is just the behavioral wrapper. The actual brain of your agent comes from the knowledge base you connect to it. Make sure to upload your internal wikis, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and historical support tickets.
