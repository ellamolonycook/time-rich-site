Time Rich Club · Members

If you’re here, I have a feeling we’re cut from the same cloth: ambitious as hell and driven to create something big.

And you’re arriving just in time: it’s the greatest time in history to be ambitious while being Time Rich.

I created the space I wish existed, because after I left my VC-backed startup, I found myself on the verge of burnout, lonely, and depressed in a NYC apartment in the dead of a freezing winter, too proud to ask for help and too stressed to cope.

Ever had that breakdown to breakthrough moment?

Time Rich Club was born a few months later, from a place of wanting to gather the smartest people I know to build an ecosystem of resources, community, and a safe space to show up and be vulnerable.

It’s a place where founders, creators, media leaders, and investors invest in themselves, their relationships, and their delegation system to become Time Rich.

The philosophy here is simple: real talk (lead with vulnerability) and real contribution (give first).

Much Love,

Ella Molony Cook

Chief Energy Officer, Time Rich Club
