Relationships and the client journey

Capture every contact, run every client the same way, and stay in touch without remembering to.

Contact capture Open in Claude 
Build me a simple contact system for people I meet at events, workshops and communities. Give me the columns (name, where we met, date, what they need, next step, follow-up date) and a 2-line follow-up message I can send within 48 hours. My business: [what you do and who you serve].
Client journey map Open in Claude 
Map my client journey step by step: discovery call, proposal, contract, delivery, report, review and follow-up. For each step give me what happens, what the client receives, and a short email template. My service: [your main offer and price].
Check-in schedule Open in Claude 
Write a check-in sequence for past clients at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months and 1 year. Each message under 80 words, warm, no hard sell, and gives them a reason to reply. Keep past clients connected even if they don't buy again. My service: [what you deliver].

Which personal AI agent should you start with?

Personal agents now do real work for you: your inbox, your calendar, your bookings. They overlap a lot, so don't try them all. Pick the one that lives where your context already is, give it one recurring task, and keep approval on.

Agent Made by Where it lives Start with this task Watch out for
Dots OpenAI Inside ChatGPT (set up on desktop) Email drafts, using the ChatGPT context you already have Needs ChatGPT Pro ($100/month). Not yet open to Pro users in the UK or EU.
Muse Meta App, web, WhatsApp and Mac Turning your saved Instagram posts into a list you can use US and Canada only. Trains on your chats unless you switch it off. Turn on approval for every action.
Grok Bot xAI Named "Bots" in the Grok app, linked to Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Slack A daily brief Bot (we set this up in the Accelerator) Paid plan needed. All your Bots share one cloud computer, so logins are not kept apart.
Instinct Instinct iMessage, WhatsApp and phone calls A morning brief sent by text Invite-only beta. Has sent an email without approval before.
Hark Hark Labs Its own app, where you can watch each task as it runs Travel and browser admin you want to watch it do Newer and less proven. Check pricing before you rely on it.

Three rules

  1. 01

    Start with one task you already do every week, like a morning brief or email drafts. Count how much you have to correct.

  2. 02

    Keep approval on for anything that sends a message, spends money or books something.

  3. 03

    Choose by where your context lives. Your agent is only as good as what it already knows about you.
