Connect your Notion to your AI

Point your AI at your own pages once, and it stops guessing. Pick the tool you use.

In Claude
  1. Open your Business Hub project.
  2. Go to Context.
  3. Choose Add, then Notion.
  4. Search for each page, or paste its link.
  5. Add it.
In Grok Bot
  1. Open Grok Bot.
  2. Paste the prompt below, with your own page links in place of the brackets.
  3. Check it confirms it can open all 8.
The prompt 
Save this as standing context. Read these Notion pages live before any task. They are the source of truth:
1. Business COMPASS: [paste your link]
2. Content Hub: [paste your link]
3. Content Library: [paste your link]
4. Content Strategy: [paste your link]
5. Ideas + Story Bank: [paste your link]
6. Distribution Channels: [paste your link]
7. Marketing Avenues: [paste your link]
8. Customer insights: [paste your link]
Confirm you can open all 8.
In ChatGPT
  1. Open Settings from your profile picture, then Apps & connectors.
  2. Find Notion and choose Connect.
  3. Sign in to Notion and tick the pages you want ChatGPT to read.
  4. Back in Apps & connectors, turn the Sync toggle on. Without it, ChatGPT will not search your pages.
  5. Open your project, choose the three dots, then Project settings, and put your standing instructions there.

Connectors need a paid ChatGPT plan. Skills and scheduled agents still run in Claude.
