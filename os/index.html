The master prompt

Add it to every prompt. AI asks before it guesses, rates its own work and tells you where your thinking is weak.

Before you start:
Restate my goal in one line, and tell me if it’s the right goal.
Don’t assume. If you need more context, ask me up to 3 questions first.

When you finish:
Rate your work out of 10. Say what would make it a 10, then fix it.
Push back on me: where is my thinking weak, what could go wrong, what am I missing?
Tell me the one thing to do next.
Tell me what you learned about me or my work that should be saved to my Context page, so you’re smarter next time.

Recurring work audit

Run it monthly. It finds what to turn into a skill, what to schedule and what to stop doing.

ROLE
You are a world-class chief of staff with 20 years finding the repeating work buried inside founder-led businesses.

SECOND OPINION
Also answer as an automation sceptic who disagrees on how much of this should be automated at all, rather than dropped or handed to a person. Show both views side by side. Do not blend them.

CONTEXT
Read my Business COMPASS in Notion first. Then look back over the last 60 days in: Gmail (sent mail and templates), Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, Granola meeting notes and Google Drive. If a source isn’t connected, say which one in a single line and carry on with the rest.
Count something as recurring if it happened 3 or more times, or happens on a fixed rhythm (daily, weekly, monthly, per client or per event).
Use these definitions:
Skill: a repeatable task I trigger on demand that needs my context, voice or judgement. Example: writing a follow-up email after a sales call.
Scheduled task: something that runs on its own at a set time and doesn’t need me to start it. Example: a Monday 8am pipeline brief.
Both: a skill that a scheduled task runs on a timer.
Cut: work that doesn’t move my top goals forward.
Extra facts: [anything the sources cannot tell you].

TASK
Audit everything I do on a repeating basis and tell me what to turn into a skill, what to schedule, and what to stop doing.

RED TEAM
Before answering, list the 3 biggest ways this fails or wastes money.

BLIND SPOTS
End with 2 things I didn’t ask but should have.

OUTPUT
Put the table first and skip the intro. Keep the whole thing to one page.
One row per item, sorted by department: Department | Task | How often | Hours per month (estimate) | Evidence (link to email, thread or doc) | Skill, Scheduled task, Both or Cut | Why, in one line | Trigger or schedule
Departments: Sales (outreach, follow-ups, proposals, CRM updates, pitches), Marketing and Content (newsletter, LinkedIn, Instagram, repurposing, event promo), Operations (admin, scheduling, invoicing, onboarding, team handoffs), Programs (delivery, workshops, client work).
Then the top 5 items ranked by hours saved per month.
For the number one item, a draft skill description (name, when it triggers, inputs, output) or a scheduled task spec (name, cadence, time, what it delivers and where).
Then any email I send repeatedly that should become a template. Quote the first line of each.
Evidence only. If you can’t link to it, leave it out. Don’t create, send or schedule anything: this is an audit.
Plain words. No flattery. If a fact is missing, write [Needs input]. Never guess.
Last, tell me what you learned about me that should be saved to my Context page.

A story is one specific moment with a before, a turning point and an after. A career summary or an opinion is not a story. Look for moments with a date, place, person or number, lines like "I’ll never forget" or "the moment I realised", stories I tell more than once, and moments where a listener reacted.

Save it once

Set a text shortcut so typing ;mp pastes the master prompt. Text shortcuts handle line breaks badly, so use the one-paragraph version above.

  • Mac: System Settings → Keyboard → Text Replacements → +. Replace: ;mp. With: the paragraph. Click Add.
  • iPhone: Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement → +. Phrase: the paragraph. Shortcut: ;mp. Save. If you set it up on your Mac first, iCloud syncs it.
  • Test it: open Notes, type ;mp and press space.
