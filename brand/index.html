The essence

What's the point of AI if it doesn't buy your life back?

Time Rich is the education and community that teaches women to delegate the shit they hate to AI — and run their business and their life on it. We help founders, investors, and operators build lean, independent businesses by multiplying their time with AI, without sacrificing authenticity, health, or relationships. Application-based, because the quality of the room is the whole point.

AI doesn't save you time unless you set it up right. Context beats prompts. Build lean. Stay independent. Stay human.

04 — Colour

Deep green, cream, and one pastel green

Three colours carry the whole brand. Deep green is the ground — the dark, expensive, grown-up base everything sits on. Cream is the light ground and the text on dark. Pastel green is the single accent: the stop, the CTA, the one highlighted word. Sage and moss fill in between, clay handles heat. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

01 Fluro lime is retired

The acid #D4FF4F is off the brand as of v5.0. Anywhere it still appears — old slides, old graphics, old exports — it becomes pastel green #CCEEA0. Same job, softer voice, and it stops fighting the deep green.

02 Pastel green is a fill, not text on cream

On deep green it is text, the stop, and the accent. On cream it is a fill only — for a pill, a button, a highlighter swipe. Any green text on cream drops to moss #67762E. The tokens do this automatically.

03 One accent per composition

One pastel-green moment per slide, screen or graphic. If everything is pastel green, nothing is. Sage carries the quiet structure, cream carries the reading, clay is reserved for urgency — never body text.

Contrast & access

Deep-green ground, cream text — and the one rule that trips everyone up: pastel green is a fill colour, never text on cream. These pairings pass WCAG AA; the last two don't.

07 — Voice & tone

Sound like a founder, not a brand deck

Direct, warm, slightly impatient with nonsense, and a little cheeky.

If you only read one screen of this bible, read this one. Four traits, four things we never are, and one rule that decides every sentence.

We always sound

DirectThe point lands in sentence one. No warm-up, no throat-clearing, no build-up to the reveal.
WarmFounder to founder, not brand to audience. We assume she is competent and short on time.
SpecificNumbers, named tasks, real before-and-after. "21 hours a week," never "more time."
A little cheeky"Delegate the shit you hate." Dry, confident, never mean and never trying too hard.

We never sound

CorporateNo empower, leverage, unlock, ecosystem, seamless, synergy. Say the actual thing.
HypedNo game-changing, transformative, 10x, groundbreaking. No exclamation marks, ever.
ScarceNo spots filling fast, no don't miss out. Confidence, not scarcity. The room sells itself.
VagueNo hedging, no passive voice hiding the subject, no "things." Find the noun.
The one test

Read it out loud. If it sounds like something Ella would say to one founder over coffee, it ships. If it sounds like a brand deck, a LinkedIn thought-leader, or ChatGPT with the safety on, it does not. Context beats prompts is the spine of the argument; specificity is the whole product.

The cheeky dial

Confident and warm, tipping into playful. "Steal them all." "It's not that deep." "No homework." "Delegate the shit you hate." Never mean, never cringe, never trying too hard.

Non-negotiables (binary — one break, the draft fails)

  • No em-dashes or double dashes as connectors. Rewrite the sentence.
  • No exclamation marks anywhere.
  • No hedging: kind of, sort of, perhaps, might possibly.
  • No fake urgency: limited time, spots filling fast, don't miss out.
  • No promotional inflation: transformative, groundbreaking.
  • No passive voice hiding the subject. Say who did it.
  • Specificity over aspiration. Numbers, not vibes.

13 — Motion

Motion like a terminal, not a carousel

Fast, purposeful, one moment per view. Motion should feel like a system responding — a cursor, a sweep, a reveal — never decoration bouncing for attention. Everything degrades gracefully under reduced-motion.

Thinking_ Hover the sweep → 140ms · ease-out

The cursor

The blinking pastel-green _ is the signature — loading states, the end of a headline, a "we're on it" beat. One per screen.

The sweep

Links and CTAs get a pastel-green underline or a → that slides on hover. 140ms, ease-out. Confirmation, not spectacle.

The reveal

Sections fade up ~12px on scroll, once. Fast and quiet. Never stagger a whole grid tile-by-tile.

✓120–200ms, ease-out. If you notice the duration, it's too slow.
✕No bounce, spin, or parallax. That's the hype content we're the opposite of.
✓One moment per view. Same discipline as one pastel-green accent per slide.
✕Honour reduced-motion. Every state still works with animation off.
