Questions

The honest answers

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can write a clear brief for a new hire, you already have the skill that matters most.

Is this about replacing my team with AI?

No. AI takes the repetitive work off their plate so they've got more time for what actually needs a human: judgement, creativity, relationships.

Is half this programme really about managing people?

Yes. Clear context, set expectations, a real feedback process that works whether you're briefing an agent or a person. It's what stops everything landing back on your desk.

Which AI do I have to use?

Whichever one you'll actually open. I'm not building this around one tech stack — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or something that lives in your text messages. Each model has a different superpower and we'll match it to what your business does. What you won't do is start over every time you switch.

I've been using ChatGPT for two years. Do I lose all of that?

No, and this is the bit most people get wrong. All of that memory is an asset. We move it into a shared system your other models can read too, so the quality doesn't drop the moment you use a different tool.

Do I need to hire anyone to do this?

No. If you already have one intern or one assistant, that's who we make superhuman. Half of this is about the people you already have, not the ones you haven't met yet.

I have a full-time job. Can I still do this?

Yes — some of the best people in this cohort are building alongside something else. That's the whole point of a system: it has to hold when your week doesn't.

What does it cost?

Founding-cohort pricing is the lowest this programme will ever run at. Apply, and we'll cover it on your call.
